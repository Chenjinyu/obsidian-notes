# Content
A greedy algorithm is any algorithm that makes the locally optimal decision at every step.
Second, what makes a decision "local"? A decision is local when it considers only the available options at the current step. It is based on the information it has at the time, and doesn't consider any consequences that may happen in the future from this decision.

"Greedy" isn't a data structure and it isn't any single algorithm either, but more of a way to approach a problem. As such, it's hard to become "good" at greedy algorithms, and there isn't much to "learn" about it. With that being said, this will be a short chapter mostly walking through examples. The concept of "greedy" is extremely general and the main thing to practice is recognizing when it applies. Greedy algorithms are usually very efficient, so if you are given a problem that can be solved greedily, it's important to recognize it.

The 2 properties of correct greedy (正确贪婪算法的两个特性)
A problem is safe for greedy if it has:(如果一个问题满足以下两个条件，则称其对贪婪算法安全：)
1. Greedy-choice property(贪婪选择特性): 
		There exists an optimal solution that starts with the greedy choice.(存在一个最优解，该最优解以贪婪选择开始。)
2. Optimal substructure(最优子结构特性)
		After the greedy choice, the remaining problem is still optimal.(在贪婪选择之后，剩余的问题仍然是最优的。)

# ✅ Better mental checklist (use this in interviews)
When you see a problem:
## Step 1: Try greedy intuition
- smallest first?
- largest first?
- earliest finish?
## Step 2: Challenge it
Ask:
### ❓ “Can a different choice now lead to a better outcome later?”
- YES → ❌ greedy fails → think DP
- NO → ✅ greedy works
## Step 3: Look for patterns
### Greedy-friendly patterns:
- sorting + linear scan
- “maximize/minimize current segment”
- “grow resource safely” (like asteroids)
### DP-required patterns:
- multiple competing choices
- trade-offs (short-term vs long-term)
- “try all combinations”
# 💥 The real skill gap
Average candidates:
- “I think greedy works”
Strong candidates:
- “Greedy works because this choice never blocks a better solution”
# 🚀 Final takeaway
👉 Greedy is not about:
- sorting
- simplicity
👉 It’s about:
> **proving that local optimal decisions cannot hurt global optimality**
