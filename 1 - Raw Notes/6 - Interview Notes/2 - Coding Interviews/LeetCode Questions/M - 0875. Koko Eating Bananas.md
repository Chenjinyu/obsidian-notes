# Problem

Koko loves to eat bananas. There are `n` piles(堆) of bananas, the `ith` pile has `piles[i]` bananas. The guards have gone and will come back in `h` hours.
Koko can decide her bananas-per-hour eating speed of `k`. Each hour, she chooses some pile of bananas and eats `k` bananas from that pile. If the pile has less than `k` bananas, she eats all of them instead and will not eat any more bananas during this hour.

Koko likes to eat slowly but still wants to finish eating all the bananas before the guards return.

Return _the minimum integer_ `k` _such that she can eat all the bananas within_ `h` _hours_.

**Example 1:**
**Input:** piles = [3,6,7,11], h = 8
**Output:** 4

**Example 2:**
**Input:** piles = [30,11,23,4,20], h = 5
**Output:** 30

**Example 3:**
**Input:** piles = [30,11,23,4,20], h = 6
**Output:** 23

**Constraints:**
- `1 <= piles.length <= 10^4`
- `piles.length <= h <= 10^9`
- `1 <= piles[i] <= 10^9`

```python
class Solution:
    def minEatingSpeed(self, piles: List[int], h: int) -> int:
        """
        koko eat bananas slowly, but she wants to eat them all. 
        the problem is able to be converted to, from the min eats to the max eats, which k is the smallest voulmne per hour,
        so that koko can eat all of them.
        let's say: piles = [3,6,7,11], h = 8.
        the koko can eat 1 per hour, then she takes sum(piles)=27 hours, since she can keep to eact rest of bananes in piles, until eat all of them
        or she can eat 3 in one hour, then she can eat 3 * 4 = 12 bananas. 
        if she eat 11 in one hour, she can eat all of them, but she still have 4 hours left.
        so, the problem is to find out the best k between 1 to 11. 
        certainly, the hours can be less than the number of piles, but we still want to find out the max k which koko can eat the most.
        
        Time complexity: O(n * log m)
        - while loop: O(log m)
          - for piles loop: O(n)
        """
        # the left starts at 1, because it's the min number of k koko will eat per hour. 
        # aslo, since the mid is used to divide p, the mid will be 0 in some case.
        left, right = 1, max(piles) 
        while left < right:
            mid = (left + right) // 2
            total_hrs = sum(math.ceil(p / mid) for p in piles)
            if total_hrs > h: # is to get the upper bound
                left = mid + 1
            else:
                right = mid
        
        return left

```
