# When to move notes between frequencies  
  
This is the **key habit**.  
When you review a note:  
- still confusing → move to **#review/daily**  
- mostly understand → **#review/3d**  
- comfortable → **#review/7d**  
- mastered → **#review/30d**  
  
Just **change the tag**.
